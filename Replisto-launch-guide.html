Step 1: Buy replisto.com and invite us (10 minutes)

Why: the domain is Replisto's permanent address, and Meta records it in the app. It should belong to your business. Once you invite us, we set up everything on it ourselves: DNS, the support@replisto.com mailbox and email sending.

We don't need your Cloudflare password. Why an invite instead: we log in with our own account and two-factor code, nothing gets locked for a login from another country, and you can remove our access in one click.

Step 2: Meta business portfolio and verification (20 minutes, then Meta's wait)

Why: Meta only lets an app serve the public, meaning any shop rather than a few test accounts, when a verified real business stands behind it. Verification checks your company's documents, so only you can do it. It is the slowest step, which is why it comes early.

Have ready: your company's commercial registration certificate (or tax registration, or a utility bill in the company's name).

Meta answers by email within a few days to a few weeks. Forward any request for more documents to us and we tell you what to send.

Step 3: Create the Meta app, on a call with us (30 minutes)

Why: the app is Replisto's official identity at Meta and holds the keys that let it read and answer messages, comments and stories. It has to be created inside your Meta account. On the call we tell you what to click and paste every value, so there is nothing to prepare or look up.

Step 4: Payment accounts (15 minutes)

Why: these accounts move money, so they belong to your business. OpenAI bills the AI that writes Replisto's answers; shops' wallets pay that cost back with a margin. Whish and OMT are where shops send their top-ups.

Step 5: Brevo account for sending email (5 minutes)

Why: the website sends emails itself: the link that confirms a new shop's account, password resets and low-balance alerts. Brevo sends them from replisto.com for free (up to 300 a day). The account is in your name; we connect it to the domain.

Step 6: Server account, recommended (10 minutes)

Why: the server holds shops' data: products, customer messages, wallets. The privacy policy says your business runs Replisto, so the machine should be in your account and paid by you. We install and maintain everything on it.

Step 7: Submit for Meta review, on a call with us (15 minutes)

Why: until Meta approves each permission, Replisto only works for tester accounts. The request is submitted from your Meta account, so it is your click; we prepare the descriptions and record the demonstration videos beforehand.

This happens once Step 2's verification is approved and we have moved the site to replisto.com.

What we do, and sending us keys safely

While you go through the steps above, we handle: the domain's DNS and the support mailbox, email sending, moving the site to replisto.com with HTTPS, your details in the legal pages, the server, the Replisto Instagram test account, the review descriptions and videos, and testing every feature end to end.

Why keys need care: the App Secret and the OpenAI key each let someone act as Replisto or spend your money. A key pasted into WhatsApp or email stays in chat histories for years.

  • Send each key through a one-time link: paste it at https://onetimesecret.com and send us the link; it disappears after we open it once.
  • We never need your account passwords. Keep two-factor login on for Facebook, Cloudflare, OpenAI and Hetzner.
  • If a key is ever exposed, delete it in that service and create a new one; we update the server in minutes.

Optional, any time: try the demo shop at https://javab.app with demo@shop.com and the password we sent you. It shows what your customers will get.
